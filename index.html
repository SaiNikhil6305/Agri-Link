System Architecture & Process Flow

Agri-Link End-to-End Supply Chain Flowchart

How agricultural commodities flow seamlessly from Farm-Gate AI Grading to Regional FPO Pooling and Institutional Buyer Delivery with zero middleman exploitation and transparent smart escrow settlement.

Stage 01 Farmer

AI Quality Grading & Harvest Intake

Farmer captures photo of harvested produce. Local deep learning models evaluate moisture, defects, and assign AGMARK Grade (A / B / C) with zero human bias.

AI Vision Accuracy: >96%
Stage 02 Farmer & FPO

Regional Yard Inflow & Batch Verification

Produce volume is submitted into the regional FPO queue with electronic yard receipts. Disqualified batches (Grade C) trigger immediate remediation guidance.

Digital Intake: < 5 mins
Stage 03 FPO Aggregator

Bulk Lot Pooling & Demand Allocation

FPO pools multiple smallholder batches into standardized commercial truckload lots (100–500 Qtl) linked to verified buyer purchase contracts with zero overselling.

Zero Overselling Guard
Stage 04 Institutional Buyer

Order Confirmation & Smart Escrow Deposit

Institutional buyer reviews lot provenance, quality scores, and deposits funds into protected escrow. Freight carrier is dispatched with live transit tracking.

Escrow Guarantee: 100%
Stage 05 Tripartite

Warehouse Acceptance & Direct Settlement

Buyer confirms physical receipt with digital inspection. Escrow automatically disburses: 100% direct bank transfer to farmers, transparent FPO service fee, and freight invoices.

Payout Speed: Within 24 Hours
